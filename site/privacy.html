Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026. Each product also publishes its own policy with product-specific details.

Accidental Genius LTD (trading as Kente HQ), a company registered in Ghana, operates Invoice Collector, No-Show Recovery, Gatekeeper, Gift Ledger and Zoning Watchdog. This policy explains what we collect across them.

What we collect

Account details (name, email), the data you put into a product to make it work (for example invoices, waitlists, deliverables or keyword alerts), billing records from our payment processor, and basic technical logs to keep the service secure.

How we use it

Only to run the product you signed up for, support you, bill you, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Hosting on our own servers in the European Union; email by Resend; subscription billing by Dodo Payments; in-product payments by Paystack where a product offers them; messaging by Meta (WhatsApp) or Twilio where a product sends messages; file storage by Cloudflare; product analytics by Mixpanel (EU). Each receives only what it needs.

Product analytics

If analytics is enabled, we use Mixpanel (hosted in the European Union) to understand which pages and products people look at. We record page views, clicks on product cards and buttons, and outbound links, with an anonymous identifier stored in a first-party cookie on kentehq.com and our product subdomains. URLs are trimmed to the path plus utm_ and ref parameters. We do not record form input or sessions, we do not sell this data or use it for advertising, and we honour your browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals by not tracking at all.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise it within 30 days, except records we must keep by law, such as invoices.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Email support@kentehq.com and we will respond within 30 days.
