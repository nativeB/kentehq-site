About Kente HQ

Kente HQ is a product studio based in Accra, Ghana, and a trading name of Accidental Genius LTD, a company registered in Ghana. We build small, focused software products for people who run real businesses, and sell them to customers worldwide.

Why the name

Kente cloth is woven in narrow strips on a loom, then the strips are sewn edge to edge into one cloth. Each strip has its own pattern, and the cloth only works because they are made to the same standard.

That is how we build. Each product is a strip: it does one job for one kind of customer and lives on its own subdomain. Behind all of them sit the same billing, privacy and support.

What we make

Zoning Watchdog is live for real estate investors. Invoice Collector, No-Show Recovery, Gatekeeper and Gift Ledger are opening soon. See all five products, how Zoning Watchdog works and common questions.

How we run them

Every product has a free plan and simple monthly subscriptions in US dollars. Payments are processed by Dodo Payments as merchant of record. We collect only what each product needs, never sell it, and delete it when you close your account. Read the privacy policy, terms and refund policy.
