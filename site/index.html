What is Kente HQ?

Kente HQ is a software studio based in Accra, Ghana that builds small, focused web apps for small businesses. It runs five products, each on its own kentehq.com subdomain and each built for one kind of customer. Kente HQ is a trading name of Accidental Genius LTD, a company registered in Ghana.

Who are Kente HQ products for?

Kente HQ builds for people who run small businesses and want one job done well rather than a large suite of tools: real estate investors with Zoning Watchdog, merchants in Ghana who sell on WhatsApp with Invoice Collector, US dental practices with No-Show Recovery, creators doing sponsor deals with Gatekeeper, and Shopify brands that gift product to creators with Gift Ledger.

Which Kente HQ products are available today?

All five are live and run in the browser: Zoning Watchdog at zoning.kentehq.com, Invoice Collector at invoices.kentehq.com, No-Show Recovery at dental.kentehq.com, Gatekeeper at creator.kentehq.com and Gift Ledger at gifts.kentehq.com.

What does Zoning Watchdog do?

Zoning Watchdog reads city planning agendas every hour and alerts real estate investors when a rezoning, variance or special use item matches their keywords. The alert arrives before the hearing, so you hear about it while there is still time to act. It runs in the browser at zoning.kentehq.com and has a free plan.

How does Zoning Watchdog compare to checking agendas yourself or using Google Alerts?

Checking agendas yourself means visiting each city's website, opening every new agenda and reading it for items that matter, which costs nothing but takes time and is easy to miss. Google Alerts is free and useful for news, but it reports pages after Google indexes them rather than reading each agenda as it is posted. Zoning Watchdog does the agenda reading every hour and only alerts you on rezoning, variance and special use items that match your keywords.

What does Invoice Collector do?

Invoice Collector is for merchants in Ghana who sell on WhatsApp. You type an invoice in WhatsApp, and your customer gets a pay link, polite reminders every 48 hours and a receipt. Customers pay in Ghana cedis (GHS) through Paystack, and it works in Ghana only. It runs at invoices.kentehq.com and has a free plan.

What does No-Show Recovery do?

No-Show Recovery is for dental practices in the United States. When a patient cancels, you text the open slot to your waitlist in one tap; the first patient to reply YES is booked, and everyone else is told it was taken. It runs at dental.kentehq.com and starts with a 14-day free trial, no card needed.

What does Gatekeeper do?

Gatekeeper is escrow for creators doing sponsor deals. Sponsors review watermarked previews, sign your terms and fund escrow in Ghana cedis (GHS) through Paystack, and the clean files unlock when the money lands. It runs at creator.kentehq.com and has a free plan.

What does Gift Ledger do?

Gift Ledger is for Shopify brands that gift product to creators. It follows every PR gift from Shopify order to delivery to the creator's post, and shows what each verified post cost you. It runs at gifts.kentehq.com and has a free plan.

How much do Kente HQ products cost?

Plan prices are in US dollars and renew monthly or yearly until you cancel. Zoning Watchdog: Scout is free, Investor is $49 a month or $490 a year, and Desk is $199 a month. Invoice Collector: Starter is free for 10 invoices a month, and Shop is $9 a month or $90 a year. No-Show Recovery: Front desk is $99 a month and Whole practice is $179 a month per location, with a 14-day free trial and no card needed. Gatekeeper: Solo is free with a 5% platform fee per funded deal, and Pro is $19 a month or $190 a year with a 2% fee. Gift Ledger: Starter is free for 25 gifts a month, Growth is $79 a month or $790 a year, and Scale is $199 a month. We give 30 days' notice before a price changes.

Who processes payments for Kente HQ?

Dodo Payments is the merchant of record for Kente HQ subscriptions. That means Dodo Payments runs checkout, takes the payment and handles sales tax or VAT, and its terms apply to your purchase alongside ours. Money that moves inside a product is separate: Invoice Collector customers pay merchants, and Gatekeeper sponsors fund deals, in Ghana cedis through Paystack.

Can I cancel a Kente HQ subscription or get a refund?

You can cancel any Kente HQ subscription at any time from your account and keep access until the end of the period you paid for. If you were charged by mistake or the product did not work for you, email support@kentehq.com within 14 days of the charge for a full refund. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Dodo Payments, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

How does Kente HQ handle my data?

Kente HQ collects only what each product needs to work, does not sell personal data and does not use it for advertising. Data is hosted on our own servers in the European Union. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep, such as invoices.

Which countries and currencies does Kente HQ support?

Kente HQ is based in Ghana and sells to customers worldwide. Subscription prices are in US dollars, and Dodo Payments handles checkout and local tax as merchant of record. Some products are built for one place: Invoice Collector works in Ghana only, Gatekeeper deals are paid in Ghana cedis, No-Show Recovery is for US dental practices, and Zoning Watchdog covers the cities whose agendas it reads.

How do I contact Kente HQ?

Email support@kentehq.com for support, billing questions or partnerships. We reply within one business day.
