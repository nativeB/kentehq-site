How we work

The same rules apply to every product.

Billing
Every product has a free plan and simple monthly subscriptions in US dollars. Payments are processed by Dodo Payments as merchant of record, and you can cancel any time from your account.
Your data
We collect only what each product needs to work, never sell it, and delete it when you close your account. Hosting is on our own servers in the European Union.
Support
Questions, support or partnerships. We reply within one business day.
